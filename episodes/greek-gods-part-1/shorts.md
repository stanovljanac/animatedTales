## 1. Podela sveta (izabran)

REZ: 1:27.10 – 1:55.60 (~28.5s) · od „the winners had a problem“ do „the one place nobody won.“
„then“ se završava tačno na 1:27.10, bez pauze: ako se čuje trag reči, pomeri početak na 1:27.12 i daj 2 frejma audio fade-in.

TITLE:
The Greek Gods Divided Everything Except This

Alternative:
- Zeus, Poseidon and Hades Drew Lots for the Universe
- The One Place No Greek God Won (ne koristiti ako je to naslov long videa — opcija 5 u publish.md)

DESCRIPTION:
After a ten-year war, Zeus, Poseidon and Hades had won a universe and had no agreement about who owned it. According to Homer, they settled it by drawing lots, and one part of the world was left out of the draw on purpose.

The full story, from the father who swallowed his children to the brother who drew worst, is in Greek Gods, Part 1, linked on this Short.

#GreekMythology #Zeus #Poseidon #Hades

PINNED COMMENT:
Poseidon drew the sea, Hades the dark, Zeus the sky, and the earth stayed common to all three. Sky, sea or the dead: which lot would you have wanted? Full story in the linked video ▶️

## 2. Otac koji je gutao decu

TITLE:
He Swallowed His Children. The Sixth Was Zeus

Alternativa:
- Why Cronus Swallowed a Stone

DESCRIPTION:
Cronus had been told that one of his children would overthrow him, exactly as he had overthrown his own father. So every time Rhea gave birth, he swallowed the child, until the sixth.

What happened next is in Greek Gods, Part 1, linked on this Short.

## 4. Posejdon nikad nije prihvatio

REZ: 2:15.40 – 2:40.80 (~25s) · od „Poseidon drew more of the world's surface“ do „Neither poem explains the difference.“

TITLE:
Was Zeus the Oldest Brother or the Youngest?

Alternativa:
- Poseidon Called Zeus His Equal. Then He Backed Down (blisko opciji 4 iz publish.md)

DESCRIPTION:
Poseidon drew more of the world's surface than either brother, and never accepted the result. In the Iliad he calls Zeus his equal, then backs down because Zeus is the elder. Hesiod tells it differently.

The full story is in Greek Gods, Part 1, linked on this Short.

#GreekMythology #Poseidon #Zeus

PINNED COMMENT:
Homer makes Zeus the elder brother, Hesiod has him born last of the six. Whose version do you trust: Homer or Hesiod? Full story in the linked video ▶️

## 5. Bogati (objaviti poslednji)

REZ: 2:40.90 – 3:32.90 (~52s) · od „Hades drew worst.“ do „arrives, eventually, in his share of it.“ (pre outroa i end kartice)

TITLE:
Hades Drew the Worst Lot. So Why Was He "The Rich One"?

Alternativa:
- The Brother Who Drew the Dark

DESCRIPTION:
Hades drew the worst share of the universe: the dead, and a kingdom with no sky in it. The Greeks mostly avoided saying his name, and built him almost nothing under it. What they called him instead says a lot about what he really drew.

The full story is in Greek Gods, Part 1, linked on this Short.

#GreekMythology #Hades #Underworld
