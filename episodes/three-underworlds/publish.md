DESCRIPTION:
A Greek musician, a Japanese god and seven Cherokee men all went into the land of the dead to bring someone back. Each was given a rule, and each of them broke it.

Three tellings, three continents, one door. Orpheus plays for the king and queen of the dead and is told not to look back. Izanagi follows his wife Izanami into the land of the dead in the Kojiki, a book finished in the year 712, and is told not to look at her. In the Cherokee story recorded by James Mooney in 1900, seven men carry the Sun's daughter home in a box they must not open.

The rule is different every time. The reason it breaks is not, and nobody agrees on why the same story turns up so far apart. Some think it is very old, others think shamans carried it, and some think every people simply lost someone and asked why they do not come back.
