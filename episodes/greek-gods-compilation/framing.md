## INTRO

[thoughtful] Twelve gods. [short pause] Four stories. [short pause] One family.

It begins with a father who swallowed his own children to escape a prophecy. It ends with a son who built a net no one could see. Everything in between — the sky, the sea, the dead, the harvest, the hunt, the war — is what that family made of what it was given.

## OUTRO

Twelve thrones around one fire. Some were drawn by lot, some were won in war, and some were never offered at all. They sat down together anyway.
